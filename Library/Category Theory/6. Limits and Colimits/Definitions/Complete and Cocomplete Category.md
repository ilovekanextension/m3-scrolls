**Definition.** A category $\mathsf{C}$ is called **complete** if for all small categories $\mathsf{I}$ and functors $F:\mathsf{I}\to \mathsf{C}$, the limit of $F$ in $\mathsf{C}$ exists.

A category $\mathsf{C}$ is called **cocomplete** if for all small categories $\mathsf{I}$ and functors $F:\mathsf{I}\to \mathsf{C}$, the colimit of $F$ in $\mathsf{C}$ exists.
