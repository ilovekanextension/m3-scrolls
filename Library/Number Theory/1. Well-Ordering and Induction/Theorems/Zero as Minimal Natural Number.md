**Theorem.** $0$ is a minimal object of $\mathbb{N}$. That is, for all $n\in \mathbb{N}$, if $n\leq 0$, then $n=0$.

**Proof.** By [[Zero as Smallest Natural Number]], $0$ is the smallest object of $\mathbb{N}$. Therefore, by [[Minimality of Smallest Objects]] $0$ is also a minimal object of $\mathbb{N}$. $\blacksquare$
