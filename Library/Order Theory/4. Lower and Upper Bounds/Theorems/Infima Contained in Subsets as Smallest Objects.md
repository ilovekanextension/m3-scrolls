**Theorem.** Let $A$ be a set. Let $\leq$ be a partial order on $A$. Suppose $B\subseteq A$. Suppose that $\inf(B)$ exists, and suppose that $\inf(B)\in B$. Then, $\inf(B)$ is the smallest object of $B$.

**Proof.** Suppose $x\in B$. Since $\inf(B)$ is a lower bound for $B$ and $x\in B$, we have $\inf(B)\leq x$. $\blacksquare$
