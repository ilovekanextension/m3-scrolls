**Theorem.** Let $A$ be a set. Let $\leq$ be a partial order on $A$. Suppose $B\subseteq A$. Suppose $b$ is the smallest object of $B$. Then, $b$ is also the infimum of $B$.

**Proof.** 
- **Lower Bound Property.** Suppose $x\in B$. Since $b$ is the smallest object of $B$, we have $b\leq x$.
- **Smallest Property.** Suppose $c$ is a lower bound for $B$. Then, for all $x\in B$ we have $c\leq x$. In particular, since $b\in B$, we then have $c\leq b$. $\blacksquare$
