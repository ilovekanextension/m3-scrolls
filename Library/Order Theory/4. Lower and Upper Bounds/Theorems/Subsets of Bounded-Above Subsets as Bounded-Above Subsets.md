**Theorem.** Let $A$ be a set. Let $\leq$ be a partial order on $A$. Suppose $B\subseteq A$ and $C\subseteq B$. Suppose that $B$ is bounded above. Then, $C$ is also bounded above.

**Proof.** Since $B$ is bounded above, there is $b\in A$ such that $b$ is an upper bound for $B$. We will show that $b$ is also an upper bound for $C$.

Suppose $x\in C$. Since $C\subseteq B$, we have $x\in B$. Since $b$ is an upper bound for $B$ and $x\in B$, we have $x\leq b$. $\blacksquare$
