**Theorem.** Let $A$ be a set. Let $\leq$ be a partial order on $A$. Suppose $B\subseteq A$. Suppose that $\sup(B)$ exists, and suppose that $\sup(B)\in B$. Then, $\sup(B)$ is the greatest object of $B$.

**Proof.** Suppose $x\in B$. Since $\sup(B)$ is an upper bound for $B$ and $x\in B$, we have $x\leq \sup(B)$. $\blacksquare$
