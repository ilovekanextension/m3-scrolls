**Definition.** The category $\mathsf{Pos}$ of posets
- has the following data:
	1. **Objects.** Objects in $\mathsf{Pos}$ are posets.
	2. **Morphisms.** Morphisms in $\mathsf{Pos}$ are monotone functions.
- admits the following structures:
	1. **Identities.** Any poset $A$ in $\mathsf{Pos}$ has an identity monotone function $\text{id}_{A}:A\to A$.
	2. **Composition.** Monotone functions $f:A\to B$ and $g:B\to C$ can be composed into $g\circ f:A\to C$.
