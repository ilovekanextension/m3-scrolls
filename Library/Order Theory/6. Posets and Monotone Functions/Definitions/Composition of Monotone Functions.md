**Definition.** Let $A$, $B$, and $C$ be posets. Let $f:A\to B$ and $g:B\to C$ be monotone functions. By [[Compositions of Monotone Functions as Monotone Functions]], $g\circ f$ is also a monotone function. We call it as the **composition** of $g$ and $f$.
