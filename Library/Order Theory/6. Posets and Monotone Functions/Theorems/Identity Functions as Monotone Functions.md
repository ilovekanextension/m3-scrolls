**Theorem.** Let $A$ be a poset. Then, $\text{id}_{A}$ is a monotone function.

**Proof.** Suppose $a,b\in A$, and suppose that $a\leq b$. By definition, $\text{id}_{A}(a)=a$ and $\text{id}_{A}(b)=b$, so we have $\text{id}_{A}(a)=\text{id}_{A}(b)$. $\blacksquare$
