**Definition.** A sequence of real numbers $(x_{n})$ is said to be **convergent** to a real number $x$ if for all $\varepsilon > 0$, there exist a natural number $N$ such that for all $n \ge N$, we have $|x_{n} - x| < \varepsilon$.
