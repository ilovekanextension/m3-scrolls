**Theorem.** Let $(x_{n})$ be a sequence of real numbers that is convergent to $x$. Then, $(x_n)$ is a cauchy sequence.

**Proof**. Let $\varepsilon > 0$. Since $x_n \to x$, we have $N$ such that for all $n \ge N, |x_{n}-x| < \varepsilon/2$. Choose $N$ to be the same, and let $m, n \ge N$. Thus, we have $|x_{n} - x_{m}| \le |x_{n} - x| + |x_{m}-x| < \varepsilon/2 + \varepsilon/2 = \varepsilon$. $\blacksquare$
