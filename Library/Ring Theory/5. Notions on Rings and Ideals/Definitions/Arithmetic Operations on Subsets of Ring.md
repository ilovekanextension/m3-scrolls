**Definition.** Let $R$ be a ring. Let $S$ and $T$ be subsets of $R$.
1. The **additive inverse** of $S$ is the set $$-S=\{-x\in R\mid x\in S\}.$$
2. The **sum** of $S$ and $T$ is the set $$S+T=\{x+y\in R\mid x\in S\wedge y\in T\}.$$The **difference** of $S$ and $T$ is the set $S-T=S+(-T)$.
3. The **product** of $S$ and $T$ is the set $$S\cdot T=\{x\cdot y\in R\mid x\in S\wedge y\in T\}.$$
4. Suppose that $R$ is a field. Suppose that for all $y\in T$ we have $y\neq 0$. The **multiplicative inverse** of $T$ is the set $$1/T=\{1/y\in R\mid y\in T\}.$$The **quotient** of $S$ and $T$ is the set $S/T=S\cdot(1/T)$.
