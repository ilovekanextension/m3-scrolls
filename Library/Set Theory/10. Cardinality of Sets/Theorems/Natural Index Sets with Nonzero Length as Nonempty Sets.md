**Theorem.** Let $n\in \mathbb{N}$. Then, $\mathbb{N}_{n+1}$ is nonempty. In fact, $n+1\in \mathbb{N}_{n+1}$.

**Proof.** By [[Zero as Smallest Natural Number]], $0\leq n$, so $1\leq n+1$. By definition, $$\mathbb{N}_{n+1}=\{m\in \mathbb{N}\mid 1\leq m\leq n+1\}.$$Since $1\leq n+1$ and, by reflexivity of $\leq$, $n+1\leq n+1$, we then must have $n+1\in \mathbb{N}_{n+1}$, so $\mathbb{N}_{n+1}$ is nonempty. $\blacksquare$
