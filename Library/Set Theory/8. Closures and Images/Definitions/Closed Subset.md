**Definition.** Let $A$ be a set.
1. Let $f:A\to A$. A subset $B$ of $A$ is said to be **closed under** $f$ if for all $b\in B$ we have $f(b)\in B$.
2. Let $\circ:A\times A\to A$. A subset $B$ of $A$ is said to be **closed under** $\circ$ is for all $a,b\in B$ we have $a\circ b\in B$.
