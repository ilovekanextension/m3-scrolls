**Definition.** Let $A$ and $I$ be sets, and suppose that $I$ is nonempty. A function $F:I\to\mathcal{P}(A)$ is called a **family of subsets** of $A$. The set $I$ is called the **indexing set** of $F$, and the elements of $I$ are called the **indices** of $F$.

If $\alpha\in I$, the set $F(\alpha)\subseteq A$ is usually denoted as $F_{\alpha}$.
