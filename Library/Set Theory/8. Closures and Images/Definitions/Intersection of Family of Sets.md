**Definition.** Let $A$ be a set. Let $F:I\to\mathcal{P}(A)$ be a family of subsets of $A$. The **intersection** of $F$ is the set $$\bigcap F=\{x\in A\mid \forall \alpha\in I \ (x\in F_{\alpha})\}.$$Therefore, $x\in\bigcap F$ if and only if for all indices $\alpha\in I$ we have $x\in F_{\alpha}$.

The set $\bigcap F$ may also be denoted as $\bigcap_{\alpha\in I}F_{\alpha}$.
