**Definition.** Let $A$ be a set. Let $F:I\to\mathcal{P}(A)$ be a family of subsets of $A$. The **union** of $F$ is the set $$\bigcup F=\{x\in A\mid \exists \alpha\in I \ (x\in F_{\alpha})\}.$$Therefore, $x\in\bigcup F$ if and only if there is an index $\alpha\in I$ such that $x\in F_{\alpha}$.

The set $\bigcup F$ may also be denoted as $\bigcup_{\alpha\in I}F_{\alpha}$.
